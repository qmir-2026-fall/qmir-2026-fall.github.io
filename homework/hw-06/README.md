# Homework 06: The logic of Bayesian inference

<!-- This README ships to students in the distribution repo. Keep the policy banner verbatim. -->

## The task

Open `hw-06.qmd` and work through it, as a follow-up to week 6.
An alien civilisation sends probes to random spots on Earth's land and records whether each lands in a democracy.
You help them learn the share of Earth's land that belongs to democracies: you pin down what that share is, put the four ingredients of Bayes' theorem in your own words, count the ways by hand on a four-faced globe, defend a prior, watch prior and data pull against each other, and answer their question as a probability.
This is **step 1** of the workflow, the estimand, and the logic behind **step 3**, the formal model.

The homework is light on code and heavy on reasoning.
Two helper functions in the setup chunk do the computing, and you only call them with different numbers.
Most answers are two to four sentences of prose.
Where you run code, predict the result first, then explain it.

Use AI tools to explain an error message or a function, not to write your answers.
The point of this homework is the thinking.
