# Homework 08: Bivariate Bayesian linear regression

<!-- This README ships to students in the distribution repo. Keep the policy banner verbatim. -->

## The task

Open `hw-08.qmd` and work through it, as a follow-up to week 8.
You ask one question of a dataset of 180 countries: is better government effectiveness associated with more foreign direct investment?

For the first time you run **all eight steps** of the workflow yourself, from the estimand to the limitations, and you fit your own `brms` model.
This is the shape of the take-home exam.
A final task asks you to **defend the Gauss-Markov assumptions** for your model, one by one, with a check and a verdict for each.

The starter gives you helper functions for the harder plots, so most of the code you write is short.
Before every piece of code you predict what will happen, and afterwards you explain what you see.
The data are synthetic, so the answers have nothing to do with any real country.

Use AI tools to check your understanding, not to write your answers.
The point of this homework is the thinking.
