# Homework 07: Markov chain Monte Carlo

<!-- This README ships to students in the distribution repo. Keep the policy banner verbatim. -->

## The task

Open `hw-07.qmd` and work through it, as a follow-up to week 7.
It has nine short questions about the ideas behind MCMC: why grids fail, what Monte Carlo and a Markov chain are, why the normalising constant cancels, how Hamiltonian Monte Carlo differs from a random walk, and how to judge whether a model has converged.
This is the logic behind **step 5** of the workflow, fitting, and **step 6a**, the MCMC diagnostics.

There is no code this week.
Every answer is two to four sentences of prose in your own words.
Because there is no session on 28 October, this homework is due at the week 8 session.

Use AI tools to check your understanding, not to write your answers.
The point of this homework is the thinking.

## What to submit

- Push to your `hw-07-<username>` repo in the `qmir-2026-fall` organisation.
