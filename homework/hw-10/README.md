# Homework 10: Interaction models

<!-- This README ships to students in the distribution repo. Keep the policy banner verbatim. -->

## The task

Open `hw-10.qmd` and work through it, as a follow-up to week 10.
Does EU cohesion money only turn into growth where regions are well governed?
You analyse synthetic data on 200 EU regions: the structural funds each received, the quality of its governance, and its economic growth.

Run **all eight steps** of the workflow once, for one model with an **interaction** between funds and governance, and show the result in a marginal-effects plot.
Then interpret a LOO comparison with a colleague's model that has no interaction.
This time the starter gives only a heading per step: you decide what each step needs.
